# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation.

## Our standards

Be respectful and constructive; assume good intent while accepting correction;
focus criticism on work; respect privacy and consent. Harassment, threats,
sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing
private information without permission and other inappropriate conduct are not
acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct conflicts with this policy.
They should apply decisions consistently and protect reporters from retaliation.

## Reporting

Contact a maintainer privately through an available GitHub private channel. Do not
post sensitive details or a person's private information in a public issue.
Maintainers should acknowledge reports promptly, review them confidentially,
and communicate proportionate outcomes where appropriate.
